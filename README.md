## Reusable Prompt Template

Create a [duration]-second video about [topic/product].

Audience: [target audience]
Purpose: [desired outcome]
Format: [16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1]
Visual style: [describe the look]
Tone: [describe the mood]

Plan the video with:
1. An opening hook.
2. A clear scene-by-scene sequence.
3. Concise on-screen text.
4. Intentional animation and transitions.
5. A closing message or call to action.

Specify timing, composition, typography, motion, and audio direction.
Use placeholders for missing assets and avoid inventing product claims.

## Example: Product Launch Brief

Create a 20-second vertical launch video for an AI creative platform.

Audience: creators and small business owners.
Purpose: introduce the platform and encourage viewers to explore it.
Style: clean typography, spacious layouts, and fluid transitions.

Scene 1 — 0–4 seconds:
Open with “Turn ideas into creative content.”

Scene 2 — 4–12 seconds:
Introduce image, video, and avatar creation through three visual scenes.
Use supplied screenshots or clearly labeled placeholders.

Scene 3 — 12–16 seconds:
Show how prompt resources help users shape their next idea.

Scene 4 — 16–20 seconds:
Close with “Explore AI9app” and “ai9app.io”.

Keep text readable on mobile and use a consistent visual style.
